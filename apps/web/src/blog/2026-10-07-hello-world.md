Welcome to the blog! Posts live in `src/blog/` and are named
`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. Eleventy reads the date from the filename, and the
post is published at `/posts/YYYY/MM/DD/slug/`.

Create a new one with:

```sh
pnpm cms new
```
